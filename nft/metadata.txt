Title: Dual Rhythm

Creators: Kieran, Yvan, Effie, Delfina

Tags: interactive, generative, p5js, shader, heart-rate, waves, MDS221, USJ, Teia

Description:

Using heartbeats as a medium, Dual Rhythm transforms the invisible psychological distance, sense of security, and familiarity between people into visual effects — making intangible emotions and relationships a viewable and interactive experience, and exploring the real physical reactions of intimacy and alienation.

Layered topographic lines flow like water over a deep black ground, coloured in rose, plum, and earth tones. Motion, brightness, and beat-synchronized pulses respond to heart rate.

How to interact:
- Click or tap the canvas, then drag left/right to set heart rate (0–400 BPM). Release to return to the automatic resting rhythm (~72 BPM with gentle variation).
- Space — pause / resume animation
- K — save a screenshot (heart-wave.jpg)

Default behaviour on load:
The piece runs immediately with a simulated resting pulse near 72 BPM. Waves drift continuously; each beat triggers a brief expansion and twist in the field. No setup or external device is required.

Created by Kieran, Yvan, Effie, and Delfina as part of MDS221 – Interactive Multimedia Installation, University of Saint Joseph (USJ), Macau.
